## Game of Thrones Bingo

An interactive 5x5 Bingo grid filled with memorable GoT phrases. Click cells to mark them, and get a Bingo when you complete a row, column, or diagonal.

Play now: [Game of Thrones Bingo](https://adek1252ai.github.io/Bingo/got_bingo.html)
